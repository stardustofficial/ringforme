# Privacy Policy for Ring For Me

**Effective Date:** August 2026  
**App Name:** Ring For Me (Love Alarm)

Welcome to **Ring For Me**. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and live location information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect the following information when you create an account and use the application:
* **Account Information:** Name, Email address, Profile avatar, Vibe tags, Hobbies, Favorite places, and Games.
* **Geolocation Data:** Precise Latitude and Longitude coordinates obtained via your device's GPS (used exclusively for nearby radar and distance calculation).
* **App Interactions:** Ring/Like history, match preferences, and streak counts.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly for core app functionality:
* To calculate proximity distance using the Haversine formula between nearby users.
* To trigger mutual "Ring" alerts when two users like each other and are within range.
* To display your public profile (Vibe tags, hobbies) to nearby users.

---

## 3. Privacy Controls & Ghost Mode
* **Ghost Mode:** You can enable "Ghost Mode" anytime in Settings. This immediately removes your location coordinates (Latitude/Longitude) from our database, making you invisible on the radar.
* **Account & Data Deletion:** You have full control to permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from the in-app Settings menu.

---

## 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
* We **do not sell, rent, or monetize** your personal data or location history.
* Data authentication and database storage are securely powered by **Google Firebase**.
* We do not share your exact location coordinates with other users; only calculated relative distances (e.g., "<10m" or "nearby") are displayed.

---

## 5. Security
We take reasonable security measures to protect your information. However, no internet-based transmission is 100% secure, so we encourage responsible usage.

---

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in our official GitHub repository.
